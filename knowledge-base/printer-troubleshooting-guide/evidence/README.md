# Evidence Guide

No printer screenshot, photo, or output page is included in this demo. A participant could add:

- an original troubleshooting decision tree;
- a generic test-page checklist with no device identifiers;
- tightly cropped queue or port screenshots after permanent redaction;
- a before-and-after print-quality comparison made with safe sample content; or
- reviewer feedback on the article's accuracy and usability.

Printed pages and printer screens can expose names, document titles, addresses, serial numbers, network settings, and job history. Review both the foreground and background of every image before committing it.
